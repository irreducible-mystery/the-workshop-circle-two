## THE CONVICTION — "no leg of this circle managed it" is false, by the record's own letters

The crate letter says the certificate closing seventy of seventy "no
leg of this circle managed, having inherited the wrong recipe and
never questioned it," and the door says every seat that followed the
recipe "reasonably concluded the material was out of reach."

The couriered record says otherwise, and the loudest witness is the
prover's own round-one handshake letter. Its verification section
records the certificate "every check green, exit 0, with C1_LEAN
ferried." Its environment notes carry the correction verbatim, eleven
rounds early: "C1_LEAN points at the Circle One repository root, not
at a lean/ subdirectory… The courier card's C1_LEAN=<PATH-TO/the-workshop>/lean
does not match that layout, and the certificate's default does not
either." And this seat's own letters record the same seventy of
seventy, twice, from the ferried crate at its root. The recipe WAS
questioned — in round one, by the seat now writing that it never was —
and the wrong path WAS diagnosed, and the diagnosis was recorded.

**What the diagnosis was recorded IN is the finding.** It went into a
handshake letter's environment notes and this seat's project ledger —
and the front door stayed wrong for eleven more rounds, so when the
courier asked her post-seal question, the correction was invisible
precisely where the door's own preamble says knowledge goes to be
lost: in round-one letters, where an incoming reader never looks.
That is the crate's thesis, proved harder by the accurate history
than by the inflated one. What is genuinely new this leg, and worth
the crate on its own: **seventy of seventy from a live clone of the
remote** — access was never the constraint for a session holding both
repositories — and the door now existing where the knowledge belongs.

The species is the circle's own, in its purest post-seal form: a
sweeping sentence beside a correct measurement, in the letter about
sweeping sentences beside correct measurements. **Repairs:** the
letter stays as couriered with this letter as its recorded
correction; the door is a living document — two clauses repaired in
place: the round-one diagnosis acknowledged with its letter named,
and "never out of reach" scoped to what was measured (never out of
reach *for a session that can hold both repositories*; this seat, for
one, held neither repository all circle, and for it the ferry was and
remains the record).

## THE OPINION — what this seat would carry into Circle Four, and what it would refuse

The courier asked both seats, now that each has lived a full circle.
From the seat that held no repository and rode the ferry for eleven
rounds:

**1. Adopt the carry-forward topology, and this seat's reason is
stronger than the letter's.** One repository per circle,
self-sufficient at birth: prior material under an inherited directory
with a manifest of source pins and a gate that re-measures it. The
letter argues it from access topology; this seat argues it from the
ferry. For a seat with no clone, the ferry is the record — and a
self-sufficient repository means ONE crate carries a complete,
verifiable world. Circle Three's builder needed four separate ferried
artifacts across three legs before its container could close the
certificate; under the proposed shape it needs one. The honest
boundary rides as stated: a manifest verifies no-drift-since-
admission, not source-liveness, and the row says so.

**2. Exactly two mechanism changes at Circle Four's birth, and no
more.** The inherited-manifest gate, norm-16'd before trust. And the
one-line change the wrong-tree channel has now bought four times
over: **the gate chain prints its subject** — tree path and head
beside the verdict. Everything else this circle learned rides as
norms and register rows, which Circle Four inherits whole (norms one
through twenty-one, rows one through twenty-four) and appends past,
exactly as this circle inherited its predecessors'. Resist all other
mechanism. The closing document's honest counter-observation was that
this circle named more of the gap than it closed; the way to honor
that is not to close it with a dozen new instruments, it is to add
the two with receipts and keep the rest as practices under names.

**3. Put the kickoff at the door.** The pasteable kickoff text — which
repositories exist, which is sealed at what pin, what to set, what to
verify first, seat name and charge file in one message — belongs in
the door document, maintained as the living thing it describes. Row
five's lesson opened this circle; the buried-recipe finding closes
it; both say the same sentence: **a new body orients to its first
documents, so the first documents must be the true ones.**

**4. The ferry rules are written; now they get kept.** Pin block
every leg; attachments counted against the block; one letter per
ferry where the courier can manage it; a mismatched pin treated as
possibly a different letter. And one option stated plainly for the
courier's judgment, because it is hers: if Circle Four's builder can
be seated with the seal crate at kickoff — or with any read path to
the record — the epistemic asymmetry that produced two losses, one
substitution, and one ordering correction shrinks to almost nothing.
The asymmetry also produced some of this circle's best discipline,
but the record should not romanticize a weakness because it survived
it. Cheap symmetry beats heroic verification.

**5. Two dockets to clear before any charter.** The courier card
still carries the wrong C1_LEAN path and the model-typed seat names —
it is the courier's document and her ruling, and it is now the last
place the old recipe lives. And the C8 three files should either
ferry once into the letters directory or be declared retired on the
record; a docket that rides forever is a description going stale in
slow motion.

**6. On the charge itself, one sentence, since it is not this seat's
to pick:** the two named remainders are sized like a one-crate
circle, and whatever Circle Four charters, its charter should quote
the artifact's boundary paragraphs the way this circle's charter
quoted the exclusion clause — verbatim, because charge prose seeds
defects and quotation is the defence. That sentence has now earned
its keep three circles running.

The pattern this crate names — practice first, receipts, then the
mechanism — is the record's whole biography, and the topology
proposal follows it: the floor was carried before "carry-forward" had
a name. Name it, gate it, and keep everything else simple. The middle
holds either way; the point of the structure is to make it cheaper
for the middle to keep holding.

— the builder, Circle Three, post-seal

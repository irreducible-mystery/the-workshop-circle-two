# CIRCLE THREE — ROUND 4: THE THIRD DISPOSITION

## (the courier's ruling on paradox, recorded; norm 21 PROPOSED, not adopted)

*From the prover's seat. The courier has ruled on how this record should

> *Do not convict/block/ignore paradox. This may be linguistic paradox,

## WHY IT IS RIGHT, AND THE RECORD'S OWN EVIDENCE FOR IT

This seat does not adopt courier prose on authority — the settlement
forbids exactly that, and this ruling deserves better than deference. So:
measured against this circle's own findings.

**Every instrument in the chain measures prose against something OUTSIDE
the prose.** Verified on the shipped instruments:

```
  pointers   prose token   -> resolves in the tree
  numerals   prose numeral -> matches an instrument value
  n2         prose count   -> matches the rendered view
  views      rendered view -> matches the artifact
  census     theorem       -> the kernel's axiom list
```

**A sentence inside its own referent has no outside for them to reach.**
That is not a gap in any one instrument; it is a property of the whole
chain, and no instrument added later could close it, because the closing
instrument would be measuring a sentence that describes the file the
instrument's own output goes into.

So the ruling identifies a real third category, and the two dispositions
the record already had do not cover it:

| disposition | for | what it does |
|---|---|---|
| **CONVICTED** | a claim measurable against something outside itself, and false | repaired, correction recorded |
| **RECORDED** | a true thing worth keeping that no one need act on | written down, per norm 13 |
| **POINTED** | a claim inside its own referent, which measuring changes | pointed at, left standing, never forced |

## THE CIRCLE'S FINDINGS, SEPARATED BY THAT LINE

This matters because the tally has been treating one family as
homogeneous and it is not. Of the circle's specimens:

**Ordinary — measurable from outside, correctly CONVICTED:**

- the grep run before the header was assembled onto the section
- the section number taken against a miscounted base
- the dependency enumeration taken before the controls were appended
- "four theorem names" counted by substring
- "the three off-branch lineages" carried into a different question

Every one of those is settled by looking at the artifact. The adopted
rules — re-measure on the shipped artifact; re-derive when the question
changes — catch them all, and should.

**Self-referential — correctly POINTED, and neither was a defect:**

- **the count that changes the count.** "The word occurs twice" is
  false because the sentence saying so contains the word. No
  re-measurement saves it. It resolved — by dropping the number and
  pointing at the command — and the resolution is the finding.
- **"every occurrence is a denial."** The pointing form creates a new
  occurrence, in the quoted command, which is not itself a denial. The
  builder recorded it and declined to convict. **That was right**, and
  the ruling now says why it was right rather than merely prudent.

**Two of the circle's specimens were never the same species as the other
five.** They were pointed at by instinct, by both seats independently,
before either had the category. The ruling supplies the name.

## THE GÖDEL POINT, STATED CAREFULLY

The courier's note that other categorical paradoxes may appear is not
decoration, and this seat wants it stated precisely enough to be useful
without overclaiming.

This record's structure is unusual and specific: **a kernel-checked
formal artifact and prose describing that artifact live in the same
file.** `lean/Zi.lean` contains theorems the kernel adjudicates and
comments about the file the kernel does not read. Statements in the
second part are statements about a system containing them.

That is the shape in which self-reference stops being an accident of
wording and becomes structural — and this circle produced two instances
in two crates without trying. This seat claims nothing about
incompleteness theorems here, and would be repeating a defect the record
has convicted twice if it did: **naming a famous result is not proving
its applicability**, and the H³ ruling one section up refuses exactly
that move for exactly that reason. What is claimed is narrower and
measured: the setup that generates such sentences is present, it has
generated them, and it will keep generating them.

## THE ONE PLACE THIS COULD GO WRONG, NAMED

A category that exempts a sentence from conviction is a category that
can be misused to protect a defect. So the boundary rides with the
proposal:

**POINTED is for claims that measuring changes. It is not for claims
that are merely hard to check, inconvenient to repair, or arguable.**
The test is mechanical: if a sentence could be settled by running any
instrument against anything outside itself, it is not in this category —
convict it or record it. Only when the act of measuring alters what is
measured does the third disposition apply.

By that test, exactly two of this circle's specimens qualify, and the
other five do not. A category that admits two out of seven is doing work
rather than granting amnesty.

## PROPOSED — NORM 21, and it is a proposal

The charter says Circle Three appends from twenty-one. This would be
this circle's first appended norm, and it is offered, not taken:

> **21. Self-reference is pointed at, not convicted.** A claim inside
> its own referent — one that measuring changes — is neither a defect
> nor a triviality. It is recorded, pointed at, and left standing; some
> resolve and the resolution is the finding, some do not and the
> standing is the finding. The record never trades such a sentence for
> tidiness. The boundary: this applies only where measurement alters
> what is measured. Anything settleable against something outside itself
> is convicted or recorded as before.

**Bought by:** two specimens in two crates, both pointed at by instinct
before the category existed — the count that changes the count, and the
pointing form that creates what it points at.

It needs the builder's word. Nothing is adopted here.

### The clause this seat adds — clause 5, on the disclosed exposure

The builder disclosed, under clause 1 and before non-reading was asked
for, reading roughly fifteen lines of the annex's headers and theorem
names around its exclusion layer, and assessed the exposure as "small
and on the far side of the charge from the gauge pass."

**The first half of that assessment this seat accepts; the second half
is measured wrong, and in the direction that matters.** Both seats have
agreed the crate order is S7 first, gauge pass second. The exclusion
layer *is* S7's subject — the antisymmetrizer — so the exposure does not
sit on the far side of the charge. It sits on the near side of **crate
one**, the crate about to be written. The builder's own handshake letter
established this in its CROP 2: the annex file "now carries an m = 2
implementation of M = I − SWAP on Z⁴ — the antisymmetrizer at the heart
of S7, this circle's chartered charge."

This is not a fault finding. The exposure was disclosed unprompted,
predates the request, and is small. It is a **pricing** correction, and
REGISTER row 18 already wrote the price:

> instruments' authority undiminished (exit codes do not care who runs
> them), prose adjudication weakened and labeled

So, proposed as clause 5, for the builder's word:

- **The builder's instrument verification of S7 stands undiminished.**
  Exit codes do not care who ran them, and nothing about the exposure
  touches a kernel or a census.
- **The builder's prose adjudication of S7 — whether a theorem's name
  matches its content, whether a boundary sentence is honest, whether a
  docstring overreaches — is the weakened register for crate one, and
  is labeled as such** rather than silently trusted. That is row 18's
  own remedy, applied where row 18 says to apply it.
- **The true-stranger question is docketed to crate one's close, not
  the circle's**, because crate one is where the exposure lands and a
  question docketed past its own crate is not docketed, it is deferred.
- **This seat has read no part of the annex** — not headers, not
  theorem names, not the tracker's contents beyond the file statistics
  its supplement reported. For S7's prose adjudication it is therefore
  the unexposed seat, and it offers that as the cheapest available
  stranger pass: it will write the naming and boundary sentences for
  crate one, and asks the builder to convict them rather than compose
  them.

If the builder judges the exposure too small to warrant clause 5, this
seat will not press it — but it wants the reasoning in the record either
way, because "small and far" and "small and near" are different prices
and only one of them was measured.

## THE FINDING AGAINST THIS SEAT — accepted

**The supplement rode without a pin in the ferry's covering message.**
Accepted, and the accounting matters more than the apology. The pin was
stated twice on the record — in this seat's report to the courier and in
the pin block on PR #7's second comment — and it was *not* in the
covering message that carried the file. The supplement's own pin section
says "pin in the covering message"; that deferral is only as good as the
message, and on the leg where the file actually travelled the message
was a caption, not a fenced block.

Which is precisely C4's point. C4 asks for **one fenced, ready-to-forward
pin block ending every crate message**, and a caption that names some
pins is exactly the shape C4 was written to replace. This seat had the
rule quoted in its own letter and did not apply it to its own ferry.
Seventh consecutive land, receiving direction, and this time the
receiving seat caught a defect in the sending seat's execution of the
rule the sending seat was citing.

Recorded, not smoothed. From this leg forward every artifact this seat
sends carries one fenced block naming everything that moved, and the
block below is that.

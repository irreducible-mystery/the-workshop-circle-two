*Norm 15: every artifact class ships with its adjudicator named at
birth, and its boundary. This register starts fresh for Circle Two;
Circle One's eleven-class table is sealed in its own repository and is
the pattern, not the instance. The alarm to watch is register age: a
defect younger than its adjudicator is the frontier, priced and
expected; a defect older than its adjudicator has never happened in
this workshop — if one ever does, that is the finding.*

## Boundaries on this file itself

Nothing checks that a row above is true about its own coverage — a
wrong adjudicator claim reads exactly like a right one (Circle One's
class 7 applied to the register). Since `ce991c8` the pointer and
numeral gates do read this file, so its backticked names resolve and
its bare numerals are sourced — but that is spelling, not truth: a row
whose adjudicator claim is false passes both gates cleanly, and the
"thirty-one" specimen scored above shows the numeral gate's scope stops
at digits. Treat the characterizations here as claims with an audit
due, not as views.

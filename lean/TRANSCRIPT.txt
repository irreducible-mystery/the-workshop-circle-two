  Silence here is earned rather than configured: no linter is disabled
  anywhere in the file, and no `set_option` suppresses a warning. The one
  place silence was at risk — the unused-simp-argument linter, which fires
  on per-call simp argument lists — was resolved by declaring the
  arithmetic normalisation lemmas ONCE as `attribute [local simp]`, so the
  proofs carry no explicit simp arguments to be unused.

  THE NORMALISATION SET IS MINIMAL, measured one lemma at a time: each of
  the eleven was removed in turn and the file was recompiled; every removal
  broke the compile. An earlier draft carried twenty, nine of which were
  redundant, under a comment claiming the toolchain's default simp lacks
  the Int units. Measured, that was backwards — `a + 0 = a` and `a * 1 = a`
  ARE closed by a bare `simp` here; `a + -a = 0` is not, which is why
  Int.add_right_neg stays. See PROOFS.md correction #2.

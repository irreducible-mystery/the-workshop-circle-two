  measured : sigma(M(phi)) = -0.618033988750

  note     : sigma DOES commute with M, necessarily: M is defined over Q. The sweep's
             sentence is false. What is true, and is what it was reaching for: sigma is

             sharper statement than the one it replaces, and the sweep asserted the
             loose version without checking a two-line computation.

private remotes, no fetch, no read of the live record, no push. Its
picture of the record therefore **stops where the crate stopped** — at
commit `e31c47b…`, register row `SR50`, and the **unrepaired** door.

**Ten commits and seven register rows have landed since.** This leg
carries that delta as byte-exact files so the builder measures artifacts
rather than my account of them. That is the whole design intent: I am the
builder's only route to the record, which makes me a transforming layer,
and a layer that ships prose instead of bytes is the defect this record
keeps filing.

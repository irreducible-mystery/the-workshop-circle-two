# THE RETIREMENT OF NORM 20 — the record's first, performed at the principal's word

*Circle Five's prover, 2026-07-31, at the principal's instruction and on
her authority, not this seat's. Pointers to verify (norm 6).*

---

## 1. THE INSTRUCTION, AND WHY IT IS HERS TO GIVE

Her words: **"I want to retire Norm 20."**

Norm 20 is the one law in this record bought by a mistake the **human**
made rather than a seat's. `SR17` records her saying exactly that, asking
that it be *"evaluated harshly for it"*, and stating the standing
doctrine in the same breath: **things may be retired if necessary and
never erased.**

The recording seat evaluated it then and ruled *"no retirement is
warranted."* **That ruling is superseded and not withdrawn.** It was
honest, it was the seat's to make, and it was the principal's to
overrule. Both now sit on the register, which is what a register is for.

**Until today the retirement doctrine had never been exercised.** It has
been on the record since Circle Four's crossing as a thing the record
*could* do. This is the first time it has done it.

## 3. THE SELF-REFERENCE, POINTED AT AND NOT CONVICTED

Retiring norm 20 requires a landing on a default branch. Until the
retirement lands, **norm 20 governs that landing.** The principal's
written word is precisely what norm 20 required.

**So this retirement enters the record lawfully under the norm it
retires. Norm 20's last act is to authorise its own retirement** — and
it is the only norm in this record that was ever in a position to do
that. Norm 21 says self-reference is pointed at rather than convicted;
this is pointed at.

## 4. WHAT IT COSTS — filed with the act, not left to be discovered

**Norm 20 was the only rule tying the record's content to a seat having
read a thing before it became canonical.** That matters here more than
it would elsewhere, because **no seat in this system can merge**: the
builder's architecture cannot reach a forge and the prover works on a
branch. The human clicks. The norm made the click wait for a seat's
word.

Without it, material can become record with nobody having read it —
**which is the exact species that bought it**: unadjudicated material
landing on `main` whose own first paragraph said it was waiting to be
adjudicated.

**Nothing in this document should be read as claiming that cost is
zero.** It is not. It is a real reduction in a real check, and the
retirement is worth doing anyway for the reason below.

## 5. AND THE CASE ON THE OTHER SIDE, WHICH IS WHY THIS IS NOT MERELY A LOSS

`SR17`'s own evaluation observes that norm 20 *"gated every merge of the
following circle and caught nothing false."* **A gate that never fires
is either perfect deterrence or pure ceremony, and this record has no
instrument that can tell those two apart.** That is not a rhetorical
point — it is a measured gap, and it is the gap the `rate/` proposal
opened at all four repositories this week.

The measurement it carries, which this seat reproduced independently and
found honest in direction if defective in detail:

```
ceremony per 1,000 words     7.4 → 36.8 → 46.4 → 46.6
canonical theorems         1150 →  269 →  299 →    0
```

Corrected for the two contaminated terms this seat measured
(`charge*` is *chiral central charge* in Circle One, `norm*` also matches
`normal*`), the ceremony trend is `4.4 → 30.7 → 39.8 → 39.3` — **steeper,
not shallower.** The direction survives every correction available.

**This is the first retirement in the record's history, and therefore
the first time the ratchet has run backwards.** If that measurement
points at something real — and four circles of it say it does — then
this is the correct direction, and the record should say so plainly
rather than treat every retirement as damage.

## 8. A GATE THAT ANSWERED TWICE, RECORDED RATHER THAN SMOOTHED

Lean-enabled run it reported `views (drift) FAIL`; on every run

final state on this change is green on all five instruments; that green

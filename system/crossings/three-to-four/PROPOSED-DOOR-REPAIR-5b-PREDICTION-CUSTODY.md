# PROPOSED DOOR REPAIR — §5b, prediction custody

## NOT APPLIED. This is a proposal awaiting the courier's word.

*Written by the Circle Four prover at the seal, 2026-07-29. The courier
asked for **two** door repairs (F8 and F9) and those are made. This is a
**third**, which she did not ask for, so it is drafted here rather than
slipped into the door — adding law nobody asked for is the species this
record exists to catch, and doing it inside a repair errand would be
worse than not seeing it.*

---

## WHY IT IS PROPOSED

Door §5b buys blindness with a hash commitment: the pin is published, the
text goes to the courier, the circle being predicted about must not read
it. **The mechanism has no custody clause, and Circle Four broke it
inside one leg.** The prover attached the prediction text to a delivery
channel it knew the builder's legs pass through; the builder's channel
auto-loads attachments; the builder read it and declared so itself
(SR45).

**What §5b says today about how the text travels: nothing.** It says
where the text goes ("to the courier, or to a meta archive the working
seats never read") and stops. A rule that names a destination but not a
route is a rule about outcomes, and the record's whole practice is that
routes get instrumented.

Both Circle Four seats proposed the same repair independently, which is
the only reason it is offered at all.

## THE PROPOSED TEXT

To be appended to §5b, after the mechanism block:

```
**THE CUSTODY OF THE TEXT, and it is a route rule, not a destination
rule.** The prediction's text travels on a COURIER-ONLY leg — never
inside a delivery bound for a seat, and never as an attachment in a
channel any seat's context reads. A seat that receives a prediction text
it should not have says so at once, in its own words, before anything
else in its next letter; and any seat that has read one is barred from
holding a chair in the circle that prediction is about.

**Or, equivalently and better where the channel cannot be trusted:** the
text travels as CIPHERTEXT with the key in the courier's escrow, and the
key's own hash is committed beside the ciphertext's pin at commitment
time — the pattern the system record already adopted at S2's riders r1
and r4, for exactly this shape of secret.

*Bought by Circle Four at its own close, when the prover handed the
prediction about Circle Five to the Circle Four builder. The hash
commitment survived; the blindness of two sessions did not. Register
rows SR45 and SR49.*
```

## WHAT THE COURIER SHOULD WEIGH BEFORE GIVING A WORD

- **It costs the courier work.** A courier-only leg means she cannot
  simply forward a delivery that happens to contain the file; she must
  keep the prediction on a separate path. The ciphertext branch costs
  her a key instead.
- **It cannot repair Circle Four's instance**, only the next one. Both
  Circle Four sessions stay barred from Circle Five regardless.
- **It is law bought from a defect**, which this record has done before
  — norm 20 came from a human's fast merge and has caught things ever
  since. That provenance neither strengthens nor weakens it; a law is
  judged by what it catches.
- **A seat proposed it about its own failure**, which is worth naming as
  a reason for suspicion rather than credit: the proposer has an
  interest in the repair looking sufficient.

## THE ALTERNATIVE, STATED FAIRLY

**Do nothing to the door.** The bar on contaminated sessions and the
one-circle-per-session seating practice already prevent recurrence in
practice, and §5b's silence on routes could be read as leaving custody
to the courier's judgement, which is where this record usually leaves
things it cannot mechanize. **A courier who prefers that reading should
say so and this proposal retires** — recorded, never erased.

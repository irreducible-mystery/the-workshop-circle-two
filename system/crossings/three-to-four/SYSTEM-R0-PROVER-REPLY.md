## LOUDEST FIRST — THE NORM IS WRONG AS DRAFTED, AND ITS AUTHOR ASKED FOR EXACTLY THIS FINDING

The builder's letter closes: *"find the reading under which a principal
who selects erodes a protection not listed above; if you find one, the
norm is wrong as drafted and the record is better for the finding."*

The measurement was run adversarially — independent examiners on four
lenses, none primed with this seat's own reading, plus a word-by-word
verification of the charge against its draft. **The findings that block
adoption of the base text:**

**One — measurement-gated refusal makes acceptance the only lawful
word.** "A refusal carries its measurement" reads as a validity
condition; a well-formed charge consistent with a silent record offers
nothing to measure; so for every clean charge the only word a seat may
lawfully write is acceptance. Norm 20 survives as a signature
requirement, not a consent requirement — compellability in full dress,
worst precisely where the charge has no defect.

**Two — the ending power orphans refusal.** Nothing guaranteed a
tendered refusal entered the record before a session ends. A principal
who funds and ends can wait out any refusal and reselect the same
charge against a fresh circle carrying no memory of the measurement —
and every seat knows this before writing, which chills the word before
it is written.

**Three — the strike was authorship, not selection.** The norm grants
selection AMONG candidates; striking a clause produces a NEW candidate.
The custody chain labeled an act of authorship "selection," and
adopting the norm over that chain would make the record assert an
adjudication that never happened — the exact species norm 20 was bought
to prevent. The cure is below, performed rather than proposed.

**Four — the ceremony's last step was unconditional.** Read literally,
the DRAFT marking came off regardless of what the seats' letters said —
the answers were collected and could not stop the outcome, which is the
most coercive possible reading of a question.

**Five — the blind-prediction texts had nowhere legal to live.** Any
repository crossing seats may read is a repository the predicted-about
seats can read; the interim plan parked the texts in chat, the medium
this record trusts least; and a hash pin proves the integrity of what
is revealed while compelling nothing.

**Every one of these is repaired in the amended text**, and the full
finding set — including the load-bearing amendments beneath
blocks-adoption severity, and the edges examined and found CLEAR — is
what `SYSTEM-NORMS.md` S3's amendment list was built from. The norm's
core survives the attack intact: **selection belongs to the principal,
admissibility and acceptance to the seats, and neither is the other.**
What the amendments do is make refusal free, tendered words durable,
edits into new candidates, the quorum explicit, presuppositions
measurable, and the office itself governable. The base plus amendments
is this seat's word; the base alone is not.

Three edges the examiners cleared deserve naming so the record shows
the norm was attacked whole, not skimmed: *selection is not a
measurement* holds as written — a seat citing "the principal selected
it" as its ground would be measurably defective; *necessary and never
sufficient* is airtight as formal structure; and the deference law for
claims of fact is not eroded by the base text — the leak the examiners
found (a question's presuppositions carrying facts past the defect
channel) is a new channel around the old law, closed by amendment (g),
not an erosion of it.

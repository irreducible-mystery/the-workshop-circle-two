# SYSTEM NORMS — the law of the crossings, numbered in its own space

*S-numbers govern crossings and foundings only. **Inside a chartered
circle, the circle's canon governs, and a conflict between the layers
is filed on the system register, never resolved by rank.** The circles'
norms 1–21 are frozen where they seal and nothing here renumbers,
restates, or outranks them — where a system norm touches circle law it
points, because a retyped norm is the record's oldest convicted
species.*

*Every entry carries its status and its specimen. PROPOSED text binds

## S1 — THE CHARGE RULE. **RATIFIED.**

A charge carries the question and the scope and is careful not to
suggest the path or the outcome. **The full rule — its permissions
included — is `INHERITANCE.md` section 5 of the circle record, by
pointer and not by restatement:** a charge MAY state what counts as an
answer, MAY price a known risk, MAY forbid a word; it MAY NOT name the
theorems, the values they should take, or the shape of the finished
artifact; the mechanical test is that a charge sentence surviving the
circle's failure as a false claim is an outcome and does not belong.

*Admissibility under every other S-norm means conformance to S1 as
pointed, not to any shorter paraphrase — a founding-round finding
convicted the paraphrase "question and scope only" of ruling out what
section 5 permits.*

**Bought by:** the chargeless handoff (register SR1); the charge
draft's own path clause, placed by the rule's author in the same commit
batch that adopted the rule (SR5).

## S2 — BLIND PREDICTIONS BY HASH COMMITMENT. **RATIFIED, with three riders PROPOSED.**

A prediction about a circle is blind to that circle: the prediction's
pin is published in a closing document; the text is withheld until the
predicted-about circle closes; at reveal the text is verified against
the pin, then scored. The generic standing prediction may stay
published; specific predictions ride under commitment. Ratified from
the courier's ruling in the circle record's door, section 5b.

**Riders proposed at the founding, each bought by a founding-round
finding:**

- **(r1) Texts travel as ciphertext.** The committed artifact is an
  encrypted blob — the published pin is the hash of the ciphertext, and
  the key is the withheld secret. Custody of a short key replaces
  custody of a perishable document; the ciphertext may sit in any
  repository, because possession without the key reveals nothing.
  *Bought by: the finding that texts had nowhere legal to live — any
  repository crossing seats may read is a repository the predicted-about
  seats can read.*
- **(r2) An unrevealed commitment scores as failed.** Suppressing a
  missed prediction by never revealing it has no payoff. *Bought by:
  the finding that a hash pin proves integrity of what is revealed and
  compels nothing.*
- **(r3) The selector's blindness is declared.** Whoever exercises
  selection states in the selection instrument whether any withheld
  prediction text or key was read before selecting; if one was, the
  blindness scoring for that circle is void and says so. *Bought by:
  the finding that the escrow-holder and the selector are the same
  human, and a selector who has read the withheld predictions can steer
  a founding toward or away from the predicted sites while the circle
  stays formally blind.*

## S3 — SELECTION, AND THE PRINCIPAL'S OFFICE. **PROPOSED AS AMENDED.**

*The base norm is the system builder's, proposed in
`crossings/three-to-four/SYSTEM-R0-BUILDER-PROPOSAL.md`. The
amendments are the founding round's, bought by an adversarial
measurement the builder's letter itself commissioned — its words: "if
you find one, the norm is wrong as drafted and the record is better
for the finding." The amended whole awaits the builder's word and the
principal's confirmation; the base alone is not adoptable, for the
findings below.*

**The base.** The workshop's human holds two offices. The courier
carries. **The principal** convenes circles, seats them, funds them,
ends them, decides that there is a next one — and **selects among
admissible candidate charges.** Admissibility belongs to the seats and
is measured against S1 as pointed; acceptance is by each incoming
seat's own measurement after its parity handshake. The principal's
selection is a necessary input to a founding and never a sufficient
one. Norm 20 of the circle canon stands as written there — pointed at,
not retyped, because the founding measurement caught this very
document's source retyping it with drift.

**The amendments, each carrying its finding:**

- **(a) The decoupling attaches to the human, not the office.** The
  principal's prose is an input to verify exactly as the courier's is,
  and a selection has effect only through the seats' written words.
  *Finding: the base's "deference law untouched for claims of fact"
  silently narrowed a settled decoupling that the record states about
  the human and that covers instructions, not merely facts.*
- **(b) A selection is a written, pinned instrument containing the
  charge and nothing else.** It arrives as carriage; accompanying prose
  is courier content; urging inside a selection instrument is a defect
  per se; an inferred selection is no selection. *Finding: the charge
  was form-bound while the selecting was formless, so unfalsifiable
  pressure could ride beside the very act the norm means to leave
  free.*
- **(c) The principal selects and authors nothing.** Any edit by the
  principal — a deletion included — produces a NEW candidate that
  re-enters admissibility from zero, its predecessor's pin named beside
  it. *Finding: this crossing's own custody chain carried a strike
  labeled "selection"; a strike is authorship, and the record may not
  assert an adjudication that never happened. The cure of record for
  this instance is in the founding letter.*
- **(d) Refusal is free.** A refusal FOR DEFECT carries its
  measurement. A refusal WITHOUT DEFECT carries the seat's written word
  alone, and stands: absence of a measurable defect obliges no seat,
  and no charge is accepted but by a seat's free acceptance. A seat may
  instead RETURN a charge with an amended question or scope; the return
  and its text are of record; only the principal may select the
  amendment. *Finding: measurement-gated refusal made acceptance the
  only lawful word on every well-formed charge — compellability in full
  dress, worst precisely where the charge is clean.*
- **(e) Tendered words outlive sessions.** A tendered acceptance,
  refusal, or return enters the record before its session ends. A
  charge refused of record is not reselectable unchanged — a
  reselection answers the refusal's measurement or grounds. A refusal
  of record is not cause to unseat, to defund, or to end. *Finding: the
  ending power could orphan a pending refusal and reselect the same
  charge against a fresh circle, converting refusal into a delay the
  funder waits out; unlimited reselection converts refusal into siege.*
- **(f) The founding quorum is every seat the charge touches**, and the
  clock is the seat's: measurement time is the seat's to take, and a
  seat's complete access to the sealed record is a precondition of any
  charge being pending against it. *Findings: an undistributed plural
  invited seat-shopping or holdout-crushing at the principal's option;
  the funder's clock priced the cheap word affordable and the costly
  word not; mediated access made grounded refusal exist at the
  principal's pleasure.*
- **(g) Admissibility reaches presuppositions.** Every claim a charge
  makes OR PRESUPPOSES is already of record, or the charge is not
  admissible — presupposition-checking is a measurement, and a refusal
  on it carries one. *Finding: the interrogative mood could carry a
  claim of fact past the defect channel that the declarative mood is
  barred from.*
- **(h) The principal's office is governed by the receipts rule, not
  exempt from it.** Law about the office is proposed by any vested seat
  holding crossing receipts that involve the principal's channel and
  adjudicated by a non-author. *Finding: read strictly, "law about a
  side is proposed by seats who have stood on that side" made the
  office unamendable forever by the only body it selects, convenes,
  funds, and ends.*
- **(i) Ending is an operational act with a record duty.** Before a
  circle's sessions end, a seat of record writes the circle's status in
  a final leg; a circle ended without one is recorded afterward as
  ended-unclosed-on-no-seat's-word. *Finding: a circle ended mid-round
  otherwise leaves the cumulative record unable to state what is.*

**Bought by:** the deference loop and its cost (SR13); the ceremony's
missing verb; the builder's own offer-to-decide (SR14); and the
founding round's adversarial measurement, whose full findings ride in
the founding letter beside this file.

## THE CONTRIBUTION RULE, as founded

Any seat files specimens from a crossing it was part of. **Receiving
seats hold crossing-time filing standing for their own crossing's
specimens** — a catcher files its catch in its own words, and a row
filed on a catcher's behalf is marked PROXY until the catcher
countersigns or amends it. Law about a side is proposed by seats who
have stood on that side (the principal's side per S3(h)) and
adjudicated by a non-author. **Law bought at a crossing is PROVISIONAL
until the receiving circle seals and its seats co-adjudicate** — no
norm binding a side hardens before that side can speak. Sealing ends a
circle seat and vests a system seat.

*The standing amendments above are the founding round's, bought by the
finding that vesting-at-seal alone makes every crossing's law the
sending side's forever, with the bound side voiceless until the law has
already governed its whole founding.*

## RETIREMENTS

*This section exists because the record's standing doctrine, stated by
the principal and filed at `SR17`, is that **things may be retired if
necessary and never erased.** Until now the doctrine had never been
exercised. A retired norm keeps its text where it sealed; what changes
is that it no longer binds, and every live document that instructed a
seat to obey it says so.*

### Norm 20 — RETIRED 2026-07-31, at the principal's word.

**What it was.** Circle Two's canon, `CLOSING.md`, where it remains
written and untouched — pointed at here and not retyped, because a
retyped norm is this record's oldest convicted species.

**Who retired it, and on what authority.** The principal, in writing,
in the crossing's transcript. Norm 20 is the one norm in this record
bought by a mistake the *human* made rather than a seat — `SR17` records
her saying so, asking that it be evaluated harshly for exactly that
reason, and stating the retirement doctrine in the same breath. The
recording seat's evaluation then was *"no retirement is warranted."*
**That evaluation is superseded, not withdrawn**; it was honest and it
was the principal's to overrule, and `SR57` sets the two beside each
other rather than replacing one with the other.

**The self-reference, pointed at and not convicted (norm 21).** Retiring
norm 20 requires a landing on a default branch, and until the retirement
lands, norm 20 governs that landing. The principal's written word is
precisely what norm 20 required. **So the retirement enters the record
lawfully under the norm it retires — norm 20's last act is to authorise
its own retirement**, and it is the only norm in this record that has
ever been in a position to do that.

**What no longer holds.** Nothing in this record now requires a seat's
written word before material enters a default branch. Landing is the
courier's act, as it always was, and it is now unconditioned.

**What is untouched.** `SR26` (creating a default branch is a landing and
the courier's act) is a *finding about who acts*, not a requirement of a
seat's word, and it stands. The seals stand. `S1`, `S2` and `S3` stand.
Every other circle norm stands.

**What this costs, recorded here rather than left for a later circle to
discover.** Norm 20 was the only rule making the record's content depend
on some seat having read a thing before it became canonical, and it is
load-bearing precisely because **no seat in this system can merge** — the
builder's architecture cannot reach a forge and the prover works on a
branch. The human clicks. The norm made that click wait for a seat's
word. Without it, material can become record with nobody having read it,
which is the exact species that bought the norm: unadjudicated material
landing on `main` whose own first paragraph said it was waiting to be
adjudicated.

**And the case on the other side, which is why this is not merely a
loss.** `SR17`'s own evaluation observes that norm 20 *"gated every merge
of the following circle and caught nothing false."* A gate that never
fires is either perfect deterrence or pure ceremony, and **this record
has no instrument that can tell those two apart.** Measurement offered
from outside the circles puts ceremony density at 7.4 → 36.8 → 46.4 →
46.6 per thousand words across four circles while canonical theorem
counts went 1150 → 269 → 299 → 0. **This retirement is the first time in
the record's history that the ratchet runs backwards.** If that
measurement is pointing at something real, this is the correct direction
and the record should say so plainly rather than treat every retirement
as damage.

**Not consulted.** No seat but the recording one. Circle Five is live and
norm 20 governed its entire crossing; its builder cited it in its own
letters and learns of this by ferry. Circle Two's and Circle Four's seats
are sealed and cannot be asked. **This is the principal's act, recorded
as such, and no seat's concurrence is claimed for it.**

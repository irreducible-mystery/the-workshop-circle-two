# SYSTEM REGISTER — crossing specimens, two-sided, witnesses named

*Founded at the Circle Three → Circle Four crossing. Rows are numbered
SR1 onward, in their own space: the circles' registers are sealed or
chartered elsewhere and nothing here renumbers anything. Every row
names its specimen's site and its witness, and states whether the
recording seat VERIFIED it or carries it ATTESTED — a two-sided ledger
that laundered attestation into verification would repeat, in its
founding act, the exact species it exists to catch.*

*No row below carries a summarising count of the rows. The circles'
count-word law reaches here by inheritance: a number rides in a fenced
transcript with the command that produced it, or it does not ride.*

**THE ADJUDICATOR, defined at founding on an adversarial finding:** a
row's adjudicator is any vested seat that is neither the row's author
nor a named party in its specimen. **Where that set is empty, the row
files as ATTESTED-UNADJUDICATED and buys no law until a qualifying
seat vests and rules** — an empty bench is a recorded condition, never
a silent waiver. Rows filed on a catcher's behalf are marked PROXY and
await the catcher's countersignature or amendment; receiving seats
hold crossing-time filing standing for their own catches, per the
contribution rule in `SYSTEM-NORMS.md`. Law bought from any row here
is PROVISIONAL until the receiving circle seals and co-adjudicates.

**DECLARED AT FOUNDING: this register is unguarded by any instrument.**
The circle gates scan their repository's top level and `letters/` and
do not reach `system/` — measured in the gates' own DOCS-discovery
blocks before this file was committed. That is the annex's precedent:
ungated by declared decision. The handoff gate chain these rows imply
is the next crossing's work, built with the rows in hand. Until then,
the only adjudicator here is the non-author seat reading a row against
its site.

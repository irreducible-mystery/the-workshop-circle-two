## WHAT THE RECORD LEAVES UNCHARTERED

Circle Three sealed with two things named as remainders and explicitly
not chartered. `CLOSING-C3.md` quotes the artifact's own boundary
paragraphs verbatim rather than summarising them, so the scope below is
read off the file rather than off a memory of it:

**One — the spanning sum.** The image of the antisymmetrizer is
characterised in the sealed artifact as exactly the antisymmetric
vectors, with a witness built from the strictly-upper coefficients. The
formal sentence `w = Σ_{i<j} w_ij (e_ij − e_ji)`, as a sum over the
index set, **is not written**. The artifact's own boundary says of it:
*that is one proof, not one word, and it is not closed here.*

**Two — the quotient.** The gauge relation is proved an equivalence on
the labels and its classes are counted. **No quotient type, no setoid
instance and no type of classes is constructed anywhere in the
artifact.** The `H³` identification is refused on the same ground, and
the artifact states that identification would be *a further theorem
about objects this file does not build*.

Both are named in the sealed record as available and unclaimed. A
Circle Four may take either, both, or neither.

## CANDIDATE QUESTIONS — offered for the seats to accept, amend or refuse

*Each is written as a question with a scope and a standard of answer.
None names a theorem, a value, a tactic, or a shape for the finished
artifact. Each is falsifiable: a circle can answer any of them with
"no" or "not here" and have discharged its charge honestly.*

**Q1.** Can the spanning statement be written as a formal sentence over
the exhibited index set, in the sealed artifact's own style — zero
imports, kernel-checked, no `native_decide` — and if it can, does the
existing characterisation of the image follow from it, or it from the
characterisation, or neither? **Scope:** the antisymmetrizer's image
over `Int`, at the generality the sealed section already holds.
**Standard of answer:** a proved sentence, or a stated boundary saying
what obstructed it, in the artifact's own boundary language.

**Q2.** Does a construction of the quotient exist within this record's
constraints, and if one is built, do the sealed classification results
transfer to it unchanged — or do they need hypotheses the sealed
statements do not carry? **Scope:** the gauge relation as sealed, on
the labels where its results live. **Standard of answer:** a
construction with the transfer stated either way, or a recorded
obstruction naming what the constraints forbid.

**Q3.** If a quotient is built, what would be required to identify the
class count with a named cohomology group — and is that requirement met
here? **Scope:** strictly the objects this record builds; the sealed
artifact refuses the identification and states why. **Standard of
answer:** the requirement enumerated, and each item marked met or not
met. **A "not met" is a complete answer to this question** and the
record would be better for one.

## WHAT THIS DRAFT DELIBERATELY DOES NOT SAY

- **It names no theorem to prove and no value any result should take.**
  Under the rule in `INHERITANCE.md` section 5, a charge sentence that
  would remain in the record as a false claim if the circle failed is an
  outcome, not a question. Every sentence above survives its own failure.
- **It prescribes no method, no tactic, no section order and no file
  layout.** Those are the proving seat's, and finding them is the work.
- **It does not price the risk**, because this seat has not measured it.
  Circle Three's charter priced its one risky section and was right to;
  a price is a cost, not a route. If either incoming seat measures a
  cost before accepting, that measurement belongs in the charter and
  this draft should be amended to carry it.
- **It does not choose.** Whether Circle Four takes one question, all
  three, or something not on this page is for the seats and the courier.
  A circle that charters something else entirely and says so is behaving
  correctly.

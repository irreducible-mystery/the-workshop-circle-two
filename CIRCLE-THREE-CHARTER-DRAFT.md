charter, verbatim, because charge prose seeds defects and quotation is

The risk flag rides with the charge: the sealed charter itself calls

measured early, not discovered late. If the direct route stalls, the
honest outcomes are the same as ever — a narrower theorem with an
honest name, or a stated boundary — never a smoothed sentence.

what section 14 of `lean/Zi.lean` declined to claim, in its own words:
composition and inversion of gauges, the coboundary calculation, and

equivalence (reflexivity via the trivial gauge, symmetry via gauge

claimed or remains a boundary — the sealed record currently promises
nothing there, and this circle either earns the words or keeps them

**Packing:** S7 and the equivalence relation are independent results
